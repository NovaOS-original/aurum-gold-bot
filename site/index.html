Research estimate only. Results are calculated on the final chronological holdout portion; earlier candles are used for indicator warm-up, not scored. Spread, slippage and commission are simplified user-entered estimates. Contract size, tick value, lot sizing, swaps, margin and broker execution rules are not fully modelled. Next setup steps

  1. Install MT5 on a supported Windows PC or VPS and sign in to a broker demo account.
  2. Run a local Python service to read market candles from MT5.
  3. Expose only an authenticated, protected API; never publish broker credentials in this website or repository.
  4. Test data freshness, risk limits, emergency stop, and demo orders before considering any further step.
